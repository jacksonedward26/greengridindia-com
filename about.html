Who We Are

About Us

GREEN GRID INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED is an electrical project management and engineering consultancy based in Kerala, India. We manage and advise on electrical projects on behalf of our clients — design, compliance, and site delivery — and stay involved from scope through to commissioning, rather than handing off after the drawings are done. We aren't a contracting firm looking to fill our own book of work; our role is to represent the client's interests through the life of the project.
