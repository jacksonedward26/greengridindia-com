What We Do

Services

Seven services covering the full lifecycle of an electrical project, from early design through to energy performance after handover.

01

Electrical Project Management

We run the project day to day — programme, budget, and contractor coordination — so the electrical works stay aligned with the wider build and nothing falls through the cracks.

02

Electrical Design Coordination

Coordinating electrical design against architectural, mechanical, and structural design, catching clashes and gaps before they reach site.

03

Installation Management

On-site oversight of contractor installation works — sequencing, quality checks, and progress tracked against programme.

04

Testing & Commissioning

Testing and commissioning of electrical systems, with clear handover documentation delivered at project close.

05

Energy Management

Reviewing energy usage and load profiles to identify where efficiency, demand management, or system upgrades make sense.

06

Renewable Energy Solutions

Planning and coordinating renewable generation and storage options — solar, battery, and related systems — sized to the actual load.

07

Technical Consultancy

Independent technical advice on electrical scope, compliance, and options at any stage of a project, without a fixed stake in which contractor or product is chosen.
